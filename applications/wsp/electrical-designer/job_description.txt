The Opportunity
Shape the World Around You – As a member of our team, you'll be involved in shaping the electrical systems that power tomorrow's infrastructure. This role is ideal for a designer who thrives on solving complex challenges and believes in the power of collaboration. You'll work alongside a multidisciplinary team, using your Bentley software expertise to deliver high-quality electrical design packages that meet the needs of today — and anticipate the demands of tomorrow.
Your Impact
Develop detailed electrical design drawings using Bentley tools such as MicroStation, Bentley Raceway and Cable Management, OpenPlant Modeler, and ProjectWise
Create cable routing plans, single-line diagrams, equipment layouts, and panel schedules that support safe and efficient operations
Collaborate across engineering disciplines to ensure cohesive and integrated project delivery
Support construction documentation and procurement package preparation for major industrial projects
Participate in design reviews and contribute ideas that drive continuous improvement
Ensure all designs meet project specifications, safety standards, and WSP's future-ready principles
Coordinate with internal teams and external stakeholders to meet key project milestones
The Skills That Set You Apart
Diploma or technical certification in Electrical Design, Engineering Technology, or a related field
5+ years of experience in electrical design, ideally within mining or heavy industrial sectors
Proficiency in Bentley software (MicroStation, Bentley Raceway and Cable Management, OpenPlant Modeler, or similar)
Deep understanding of electrical systems and design principles
Organized and detail-oriented, with a collaborative mindset and a passion for innovation
