The Power System Controller Apprentice is responsible for supporting the operation of the electrical distribution system to ensure the safe, reliable, and cost-effective delivery of electrical power on a 24‑hour, 365‑day‑a‑year basis. In this role, the Apprentice monitors system conditions, develops and directs system switching, dispatches field crews, and administers work protection in accordance with the Utility Work Protection Code (UWPC). The Apprentice also assists in coordinating trouble response during emergency events under the guidance and direction of a Power System Controller. This position operates on a rotating 24/7/365 shift schedule.


This posting is to fill up to three (3) number of positions, at least one of which is an existing vacancy. Toronto Hydro reserves the right, in alignment with the applicable collective agreement, to finalize the number of positions filled.

 


KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:

Creates, updates, maintains and verifies logs, reports and databases to record system conditions during planned and emergency conditions
Leads, distributes, follows up on and checks work; answers inquiries and gives guidance as required
Liaises, consults and co-ordinates with contractors; internal and external customers
Operates the system to ensure the safe, reliable and cost-effective distribution of electrical energy
Monitors system loading and initiates appropriate action to achieve desired system performance
Ensures proper isolation and work protection is provided for planned and emergency work
Directs and dispatches field operations to complete planned and emergency work
Schedules, plans and directs switching operations to complete planned and emergency work
Recommends changes and upgrades to the system to improve system performance and safety
Maintains system maps, displays and schematics
Informs other departments of emergency response status
Supports and assists field crews in response to emergencies such as outages, accidents and spills
Advises management of significant events

REQUIREMENTS:

Graduation from an accredited college or university with one of the following: Electrical Engineering Technologist diploma; Electromechanical Engineering Technology – Power and Control Diploma; Bachelor of Electrical Engineering degree or Bachelor degree with a concentration on Energy Systems Engineering
Preference will be given to candidates with the Certified Engineering Technologist (CET) designation from the Ontario Association of Certified Engineering Technicians and Technologists (OACETT)
Familiarity with utility safety regulations and work practices
Demonstrated experience in a variety of business computer software packages
Familiar with computerized, remote automated switching system
Excellent communication skills, both verbal and written
Safety performance and familiarity with utility safety regulations and practices; adopts safe and efficient work practices, participates in job planning, risk reduction, and reports problems
Customer Service and Trust; excels in providing timely and effective services and exceeding customer expectations
Leadership; keeps people informed, promotes team morale and productivity
Adherence to Policies and Procedures; complies with policies, practices and procedures
Knowledge of Work; demonstrates an in-depth level of knowledge regarding the job, current role and accountabilities
Problem Solving; takes into account all relevant information when making a decision
Dependability; reliable and counted on to get the work done, ability to work with minimal supervision
Quality of Work; work is organized, detailed, in-depth, and is consistently of high quality
Volume of Work completed far outpaces the expectations; adapts well to changing volumes of work and/or pace of work and proactively seeks out additional work
