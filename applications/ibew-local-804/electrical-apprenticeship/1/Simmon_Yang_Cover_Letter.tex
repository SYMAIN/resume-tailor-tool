IBEW Local 804 Apprenticeship Committee

Dear Members of the Apprenticeship Committee,

I am writing to apply for the 309A Construction and Maintenance Electrician apprenticeship with IBEW Local 804. I recently completed the Electrical Engineering Technology diploma at Centennial College with Honours, and I am looking to build my career as a licensed electrician through a registered apprenticeship.

My coursework covered electrical theory, the Canadian Electrical Code, drawing interpretation and installation, and power generation and distribution, giving me a solid technical foundation to build on as an apprentice. Outside the classroom, I have put that foundation to use directly: as an as-needed Electrician Assistant, I was called in periodically to support a licensed electrician on residential and commercial jobs, where I pulled, routed, and terminated wiring and conduit, installed switches, outlets, fixtures, and breakers to code, and verified continuity, voltage, and insulation resistance before energization. I have also worked as a Low Voltage Technician installing and terminating structured cabling to TIA/EIA standards, and as an Electrical Technician Assistant repairing and load-testing DC-AC inverter systems. Each of these roles has reinforced the habits I understand a good apprentice needs most: careful attention to safety, willingness to take direction, and consistency in getting the details right.

I hold current WHMIS and Working at Heights certifications and am comfortable working in the field across a variety of conditions. I am reliable, hard-working, and genuinely motivated to learn the trade properly through IBEW Local 804's training program, and I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background fits the apprenticeship.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,

Simmon Yang
