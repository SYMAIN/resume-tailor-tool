About the job
Aplin is partnered with a global leader to hire an Electrical Design Engineer to join their team in Brampton, ON. This role will start as a 1 year contract with opportunity for extension or conversion to permanent.

About the Role:

We are seeking a Design Engineer to support avionics box design and electrical engineering initiatives within advanced robotics and space-related programs. This role involves supporting ongoing operational programs as well as new development and R&D initiatives.
You will work closely with cross-functional engineering teams to develop detailed mechanical and electrical design solutions for space and terrestrial applications.

Key Responsibilities:

Candidates do not need experience in every area below but should demonstrate solid CAD and drawing experience.
Prepare drawings and Bills of Materials (BOM) for:
Avionics chassis
Circuit Card Assemblies (CCA)
Printed Circuit Boards (PCB)
Cable harness designs
Create cable assembly drawings using NX or AutoCAD to established standards
Interpret client requirements and develop compliant design solutions
Design cable mechanisms for rotary interfaces
Perform cable routing and 3D modeling
Integrate electrical, materials, thermal, and structural design inputs into 3D models
Support avionics chassis design with considerations for EMI/EMC, thermal, structural, mass, and volume constraints
Develop production, assembly, and parts list drawings
Create and maintain standard manuals, processes, and templates
Contribute to internal CAD libraries of parts and product components
 Qualifications:
Bachelor’s degree in Aerospace, Electrical, Mechanical, Computer, or Systems Engineering
Strong written and verbal communication skills
Detail-oriented with strong critical thinking abilities
Able to work in a fast-paced, requirement-driven environment
Team-oriented with a high level of integrity
Customer-focused mindset
 Assets (Nice to Have):
Knowledge of geometric tolerancing and drawing standards
Experience in the space or aerospace sector
Experience with NX/Teamcenter, AutoCAD, AutoCAD Electrical, MS Visio, Altium, or OrCAD
Security Requirements:
Successful candidates must be eligible to obtain and maintain:
Reliability Status security clearance
Controlled Goods Program (CGP) clearance
ITAR compliance
