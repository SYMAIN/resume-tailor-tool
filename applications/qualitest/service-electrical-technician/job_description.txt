Service & Electrical Technician (Junior–Intermediate)
Qualitest
Richmond Hill, ON
$36,000–$50,000 a year - Full-time

Qualitest, a global manufacturer of materials testing and quality control instruments, is seeking a Junior–Intermediate Service & Electrical Technician to join our growing technical team.

This is a hands-on role ideal for a versatile technician interested in electrical systems, certification compliance, and laboratory instrumentation.

Applicants must currently reside within approximately 15 km of Richmond Hill, Ontario.

Key Responsibilities:
- Read and interpret electrical drawings and wiring diagrams
- Perform electrical modifications and internal wiring changes to support CSA, UL, and SPE-1000 certification
- Assist with repair, calibration, troubleshooting, and technical inspection of testing instruments
- Support instrument commissioning, internal testing, and final technical checks
- Assist with preventive maintenance and service activities
- Document service work and electrical changes clearly
- Occasionally travel (up to 20%) for installations, service, or calibration
- Communicate professionally with customers and internal teams

Qualifications:
- Diploma or technical education in Electrical, Electromechanical, Mechatronics, or Instrumentation
- 1–3 years of hands-on technical experience (lab, industrial, OEM, or service environment)
- Ability to work with control panels, sensors, motors, and electromechanical systems
- Strong troubleshooting skills and willingness to learn
- Good written and verbal English
- Valid driver's license and ability to travel

Assets:
- Experience with CSA, UL, SPE-1000, calibration, or laboratory equipment

What We Offer:
- Training and mentorship
- Exposure to a wide range of testing technologies
- Long-term growth opportunities
- Competitive compensation and benefits after probation

Instruments You Will Work With:
- Universal tensile and compression testing machines
- Hardness and impact testers
- Environmental and temperature chambers
- Spectrometers, XRF analyzers, and laboratory analytical equipment
