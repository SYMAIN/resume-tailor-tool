HVAC Apprentice, 1st - 5th year
Ainsworth Inc. (GDI Ainsworth)
5985 Mclaughlin Road, Mississauga, ON L5R 1B8
$23.29–$51.27 an hour - Permanent

Job Summary:
Assist Journeyman technicians in the overhaul and servicing of HVAC/R units and systems.

Key Responsibilities:
- Perform preventative maintenance on HVAC equipment for commercial customers.
- Installation of HVAC/R-related equipment and components.
- Assist Journeyman technicians in the overhaul and servicing of HVAC/R units and systems.
- Perform maintenance and repair on refrigeration equipment and systems.
- Adhere to all company policies and procedures.
- Perform all job functions in a safe and healthful manner, abiding by and in accordance with all applicable Health, Safety, and Environmental company policies and government legislation/regulations.
- Contribute to the ongoing training and mentoring of indentured apprentices.
- Adhere to the Company's Quality System operating procedures.
- Perform other duties as assigned by Management.

Qualifications:
- Currently enrolled in, or have you completed, an HVAC/R apprenticeship program in Ontario
- Basic understanding of Mechanical Installation and repairs.
- Valid Ontario Drivers License.
- Excellent Math and problem solving skills.
- Ability to work alone and in a team environment.
- Must be able to lift heavy materials, tools and equipment when required.

The pay range is $23.29 to $51.27 per hour. The compensation for this position is as per the collective agreement (union).

This posting is for an active and current vacancy; interested applicants are encouraged to apply as soon as possible.
